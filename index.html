Built by people who actually go outside

Trailhead started on a rained-out weekend when a planned loop turned into three hours of backtracking. We wanted one place to keep the route, the gear, and the map — instead of three apps and a paper printout.

Today it is a small, focused tool used by weekend hikers and long-distance thru-hikers alike. We add features slowly and only when they earn their weight in the pack.
